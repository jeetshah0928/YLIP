Questions

Before you reach out.

01Do I need political experience to join YLIP?

No. YLIP opportunities can exist for different experience levels. Genuine interest, reliability, professionalism, and willingness to contribute matter.

02Does joining YLIP guarantee me an internship or campaign role?

No. YLIP helps create and facilitate political opportunities, but placements depend on availability, fit, reliability, and the needs of campaigns, offices, and organizations.

03Can campaigns work directly with YLIP?

Yes. Candidates and campaigns can work with YLIP across campaign strategy, creative and digital communications, grassroots outreach, data and research, and event management.

04Can a government office or political organization share an opportunity?

Yes. Offices, campaigns, political organizations, and civic organizations can connect with YLIP about opportunities for young leaders.

05What kind of political experience can I get through YLIP?

Experiences can involve campaigns, government, legislative environments, political research, communications, data, field operations, events, advocacy, civic engagement, and internships.

06Is every opportunity available all the time?

No. Political opportunities change based on election cycles, campaigns, legislative activity, events, organizational needs, and availability.

Politics doesn't start when someone gives you a title.
