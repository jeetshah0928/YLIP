The YLIP Perspective

Politics looks different when you're in the room.

YLIP's perspective comes from direct participation in political environments. Each kind of work teaches something different.

Campaign work
creates lessons about voters and organizing.
Government exposure
creates insight into institutions and legislation.
Political events
create relationships and perspective.
Leadership
creates lessons about people and execution.

YLIP Insights is a place to share those lessons, explain political processes, highlight organizational work, and explore developments relevant to young leaders and campaigns.
