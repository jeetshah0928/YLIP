The YLIP Difference

Political experience shouldn't start after college.

Plenty of young people care about politics. Far fewer know how to move from interest to real involvement.

A typical political club

Discusses elections and government.

YLIP

Connects you directly to the political process.

YLIP connects young people with campaigns, government offices, political organizations, advocacy, events, internships, and civic initiatives. Members learn by contributing — and they gain experience because they are given meaningful responsibility.

Our Philosophy

Learn politics by doing politics.

Real political understanding comes from participating in the process.

  • You understandCampaignsdifferently after knocking doors.
  • You understandGovernmentdifferently after entering a legislative office.
  • You understandAdvocacydifferently after working around legislation.
  • You understandCommunicationdifferently after helping create it.
  • You understandLeadershipdifferently after being responsible for other people.

Who YLIP Is For

You don't need a political résumé to start.

YLIP is for motivated young people who are serious about politics, government, campaigns, public policy, communications, research, organizing, or civic leadership.

Experience is valuable. But what matters most is what you bring to the work:

  • Curiosity
  • Reliability
  • Willingness to work
  • Genuine interest

You don't need to know everyone in politics. You need to be willing to learn, contribute, and show up.
