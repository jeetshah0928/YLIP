Get in the Room

Interest is the start. Experience comes next.

Plenty of young people are interested in politics. Far fewer know how to find legitimate ways to participate. YLIP helps bridge that gap.

Through relationships with campaigns, elected officials, political organizations, civic institutions, and political professionals, YLIP helps connect young leaders with meaningful opportunities — built around real work, real political environments, and real responsibility.

Opportunities vary over time and are not guaranteed. Placement depends on availability, fit, and the needs of each campaign, office, or organization.

The Process

From interest to involvement.

YLIP facilitates connections. Campaigns, offices, organizations, or YLIP may determine whether someone is the right fit for a specific opportunity.

  1. 01

    Discover

    Explore opportunities that match your interests, skills, location, and availability.

  2. 02

    Connect

    Express interest through YLIP and provide the information needed for the opportunity.

  3. 03

    Match

    When appropriate, YLIP helps connect qualified and reliable young leaders with campaigns, offices, organizations, or political opportunities.

  4. 04

    Contribute

    Show up, do meaningful work, build experience, and earn greater responsibility.

Who Thrives Here

You don't need experience to bring value.

YLIP opportunities are designed for motivated young people at different levels of political experience. What matters is how you show up.

  • 01

    Reliability

    If you commit to something, show up.

  • 02

    Curiosity

    Be willing to learn how campaigns, government, and politics actually work.

  • 03

    Initiative

    Look for ways to contribute instead of waiting to be told every step.

  • 04

    Professionalism

    Treat campaigns, elected officials, organizations, voters, and other members with respect.

  • 05

    Work Ethic

    Political work can involve long days, repetitive tasks, and responsibility. Be willing to do the work.

  • 06

    Genuine Interest

    The strongest members care about learning and contributing — not simply adding another line to a résumé.

YLIP is for people who want political experience — not people collecting credentials.

No Connections Required

You shouldn't have to know someone to start.

Politics can feel hard to enter. Too often, opportunities depend on already knowing a campaign, an elected official, an organization, or someone working in politics.

YLIP exists partly to lower that barrier — building relationships so motivated young people can access political environments where they can prove themselves through their work.

Access opens the door.
What you do once you're inside is up to you.

Questions

Before you get started.

01Do I need political experience to join?

No. Opportunities can exist for different experience levels. Reliability, genuine interest, professionalism, and willingness to contribute matter.

02Am I guaranteed an internship or campaign position?

No. YLIP helps create and facilitate opportunities, but placements depend on availability, fit, reliability, and the needs of campaigns, offices, and organizations.

03What types of opportunities does YLIP offer?

Opportunities can involve campaigns, government, legislative offices, political research, communications, data, field operations, political events, advocacy, and civic engagement.

04Are opportunities always available?

No. Political opportunities change throughout election cycles, legislative sessions, campaigns, and events. This page is updated as new opportunities become available.

05Can campaigns or political offices submit an opportunity?

Yes. Campaigns, offices, political organizations, and civic leaders interested in connecting with young leaders can contact YLIP through the Get Involved page.
